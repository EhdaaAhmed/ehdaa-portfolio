# Ehdaa Ahmed - Portfolio

## How to add your photo
1. Put your personal photo inside the `images` folder.
2. Rename the image to exactly:
   `profile.jpg`
3. Open `index.html` in your browser.

If your image is PNG, either rename it to `profile.jpg` after converting it,
or change `profile.jpg` in `index.html` to `profile.png`.

## Before publishing
- Replace the `#` GitHub and LinkedIn links in `index.html`.
- Replace the project `#` links with your real repository/live-demo links.
- Add screenshots of your projects if you want.
- You can change colors from the variables at the top of `style.css`.

## Files
- index.html
- style.css
- script.js
- images/profile.jpg (add your own photo)
